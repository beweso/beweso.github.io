Our First Product

Meet Dexaroo.

The digital home for your Pokémon TCG collection. Dexaroo helps collectors keep track of what they own, what they are missing and how close they are to completing their sets.

Visit Dexaroo →

Pokédex Tracking

Search through all Pokémon, view directly linked TCG cards, and easily track your progress on the National Pokédex.

Set Management & Completeness

See exactly which cards you own per TCG set, monitor market values, and track your progress toward a complete Master Set.

Achievements & Badges

Unlock unique badges and milestones as your collection grows, from specific generations to special card sets.

Features Overview

Key features of Dexaroo

Everything you need to organize, value, and share your Pokémon TCG collection in one place.

Pokédex & Sets

Browse every Pokémon and TCG set, track missing cards, and measure your set completion.

Market Value & Prices

See real total market values, track per-card prices, and view historical price trends.

Fair Trade Tool

Compare total market value for both sides of a trade to make sure every deal is fair.

Binder Placeholders

Generate printable placeholder cards for your physical binder to keep your slots organized.

Wishlist & Selling Lists

Save cards you are looking to buy or mark cards from your collection you want to sell.

Achievements & Badges

Unlock achievements for collector milestones, generation completion, starters, and more.

Trainer Profiles

Share your public profile, customize avatar and header colors, and compete on leaderboards.

Import & Export

Import caught Pokémon from CSV/Excel files and export your complete collection as JSON or spreadsheet.
